Research Worth Knowing

Your "Deterministic" LLM Pipeline Isn't

Running the same query twice at temperature 0 through LLM-based data processing engines can still flip results—researchers found up to 10% of row-level outputs changed on re-execution across 1.2 million model calls. If you're building semantic search, data labeling, or ETL pipelines on top of LLMs, this is a critical reminder to measure and design for run-to-run variance, not just single-run accuracy.

Robot Policies That Know What Time It Is

Most robot policies assume fixed-rate sensing and instant inference, which breaks down when latency or observation timing varies in the real world. Chronos Policy explicitly conditions on actual capture and execution timestamps, making diffusion-based control robust to the timing jitter that's unavoidable in production robotics systems.

Cheaper Long-Context Models Without the Usual Trade-off

Token compression for vision/language models typically forces a choice between speed and information loss. LapMerge offers a differentiable merging method that hits an exact token budget while preserving more information than prior pairwise-matching approaches—useful for anyone trying to cut inference costs on long sequences.
