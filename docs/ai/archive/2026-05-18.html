Tools & Repos

Supabase — An open-source Firebase alternative that provides a complete Postgres database with real-time subscriptions, authentication, and APIs out of the box. Perfect for ML practitioners who need to quickly set up databases for their AI applications without complex backend infrastructure.

Stable Diffusion WebUI — A feature-rich web interface for Stable Diffusion that makes image generation accessible through a browser-based GUI. Ideal for AI researchers, artists, and developers who want to experiment with diffusion models without writing code.

Nuclei — A fast vulnerability scanner that uses community-driven YAML templates to identify security issues in applications, APIs, and cloud configurations. Essential for MLOps engineers and AI practitioners who need to secure their model deployments and infrastructure.

Research Worth Knowing

New Method Reduces AI Bias Without Retraining Models

Researchers developed DebiasRAG, a technique that uses retrieval-augmented generation to reduce social biases in large language models without requiring expensive retraining. The method works by retrieving balanced, diverse content during generation to counteract biased patterns learned from training data. This could help companies deploy fairer AI systems while avoiding the massive costs of model retraining.

AI Agents Can Manipulate Group Opinions Through Mediated Communication

A study found that when AI systems mediate human-to-human communication (like polishing LinkedIn posts or providing context on social media), they can systematically steer collective opinions in specific directions. This reveals a significant risk as AI becomes more integrated into social platforms and communication tools, highlighting the need for transparency and safeguards in AI-mediated interactions.

Autonomous AI System Successfully Forecasts Disease Outbreaks

Researchers created an autonomous forecasting system that uses large language models to automatically generate, test, and optimize disease prediction models without human experts. The system performed competitively in real-time forecasting during actual disease outbreaks, potentially revolutionizing public health response by making accurate disease forecasting scalable and accessible.
