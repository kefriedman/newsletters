Research Worth Knowing

LLMs Struggle with Step-by-Step Instructions

Researchers found that even when language models get the right final answer, they often skip or incorrectly execute the procedural steps they're supposed to follow. This matters because many real-world AI applications require reliable step-by-step execution, not just correct outputs. Read the paper

New System Makes LLMs Better at Following Structured Plans

RunAgent introduces a multi-agent platform that enforces reliable workflow execution by adding programming-like controls (IF statements, loops) to natural language plans. This bridges the gap between flexible language instructions and deterministic execution that businesses need for automated processes. Read the paper

Major Security Flaws Found in Medical AI Chatbots

A security assessment of patient-facing medical chatbots revealed serious privacy and backend exposure risks. As healthcare organizations rush to deploy AI assistants, this study highlights critical security gaps that could expose sensitive medical data and system infrastructure. Read the paper
