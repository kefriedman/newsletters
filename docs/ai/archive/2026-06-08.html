Research Worth Knowing

LLMs Struggle with Basic Probability Problems

Researchers tested 8 state-of-the-art language models on dice and probability problems, finding they achieved 96% accuracy on straightforward problems but only 59% on counterintuitive ones. This reveals a significant gap in probabilistic reasoning that could impact AI applications in finance, risk assessment, and decision-making systems. Read the paper

Better Text Embeddings Hidden in Language Models

Scientists discovered that LLMs contain high-quality text embeddings in their "unembedding matrix" that significantly outperform standard embedding approaches on benchmarks. This finding could immediately improve search systems, recommendation engines, and any application relying on semantic text similarity without requiring new model training. Read the paper

Detecting AI Hallucinations in Speech Recognition

Researchers found ways to detect when Whisper (the popular speech-to-text model) generates coherent but completely wrong transcriptions for non-speech audio. They developed methods to identify and potentially prevent these hallucinations by analyzing the model's internal representations, which could improve reliability in production speech systems.
